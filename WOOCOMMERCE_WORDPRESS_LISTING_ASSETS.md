**Full Description:**
AI Chat Support adds a fast, customizable chat widget to your WordPress or WooCommerce site. The assistant answers FAQs, product questions, order status, and business info using your verified data sources. The widget is lightweight, mobile-friendly, and easy to configure.

**Key Features:**
- AI-powered answers from verified data sources
- WooCommerce-friendly for product and order questions
- Customizable widget position, color, and greeting
- Analytics and conversation history
- Multi-tenant dashboard support
